Privacy Policy

Titan Intervals: HIIT Timer • Last updated: October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Titan Intervals: HIIT Timer ("we", "our", or "us") manages information when you use our mobile application on Android.

Core Privacy Summary: Titan Intervals does not require an account, does not collect personal names or contact lists, and stores all your workout timer routines locally on your device.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, physical location, or contact book. All workouts, custom routines, and configuration preferences exist solely on your phone.

2. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing development and keep the app free, Titan Intervals integrates the Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). Google AdMob may automatically collect and process certain non-personally identifiable diagnostic and advertising data:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID/AAID).
  • Technical Data: IP address, device model, operating system version, and general network connection type.
  • Ad Interaction Data: Impressions, click events, and ad diagnostics for fraud prevention.

For detailed information on how Google manages advertising data, please consult:

  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • Google Privacy Policy

3. Device Permissions

Titan Intervals requests only the minimum required Android permissions:

  • Internet & Network State: Used exclusively to request advertisements through Google AdMob.
  • Vibration: Used exclusively to deliver haptic buzzes when workout intervals transition.

4. Managing Your Privacy & Opting Out

You can reset or opt out of personalized ad tracking anytime directly from your Android device settings:

  • Open Settings > Google > Ads.
  • Tap Reset advertising ID or Delete advertising ID.

5. Children's Privacy

Titan Intervals is designed for general adult fitness audiences and is not directed to children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect information from children.
