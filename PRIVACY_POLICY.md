# Privacy Policy for Titan Intervals: HIIT Timer

**Last Updated:** October 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how **Titan Intervals: HIIT Timer** ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use our mobile application on Android devices.

By downloading, installing, or using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### A. Personal Data
Titan Intervals does **not** require you to create an account, register, or provide personally identifiable information such as your name, email address, home address, or phone number. All workout routines, custom timers, and local preferences are stored locally on your device.

### B. Device & Usage Information (Third-Party SDKs)
While we do not collect personal data directly, the App uses third-party services that may automatically collect certain diagnostic and advertising identifiers:

- **Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK)**:
  - **Data Collected**: Device advertising identifiers (Google Advertising ID / GAID), IP address, general approximate location derived from IP, device brand and model, operating system version, and ad interaction data.
  - **Purpose**: To serve non-personalized and personalized advertisements, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.
  - For more information on how Google uses data from partner apps, please review:  
    👉 [Google's Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)  
    👉 [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

- **Google Play Services**:
  - Provides core application infrastructure and analytics on app performance and crashes.

---

## 2. Permissions Used by the App

The App requests the following minimal permissions in the Android Manifest:

- `INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required solely for loading advertisements via Google AdMob and checking network availability.
- `VIBRATE`: Used exclusively to provide haptic feedback during workout interval transitions.

The App does **not** request access to your precise GPS location, camera, microphone, contacts, or storage photos.

---

## 3. Data Retention and Security

- **Local Data**: Any workout routines and preferences you create remain stored entirely on your device's internal storage and are removed when you uninstall the App or clear app storage.
- **Security**: Third-party transmissions (such as ad requests to Google servers) are protected using industry-standard HTTPS encryption in transit.

---

## 4. Your Rights & Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You have full control over advertising identifiers directly from your Android device settings:

- **Reset or Delete Advertising ID**:
  - Go to your device **Settings** > **Google** (or **Privacy**) > **Ads**.
  - Select **"Reset advertising ID"** or **"Delete advertising ID"**.
- Under European regulations (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), you have the right to request access, rectification, or erasure of any data processed by third-party ad networks in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Titan Intervals is intended for a general audience and is not targeted at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.
